Classical Devotional

Union and Communion

James Hudson Taylor

A short devotional reading of the Song of Songs by the founder of the China Inland Mission, tracing six movements of the soul’s life with Christ — surrender, drift, restoration, and at last a communion that no longer breaks. Offered in full, with each section followed by its Scripture passage as Taylor arranged it, marking who speaks throughout.

Sermons on the Song of Songs

Bernard of Clairvaux

Eighty-six sermons preached to the monks of Clairvaux across more than twenty years. The most sustained meditation on divine love in the Western tradition — tender, rigorous, and luminous throughout. Bernard never reaches chapter 3.

Classical Devotional

Sermons on the Song of Songs

Charles Haddon Spurgeon

Sixty-three sermons on the Song of Songs, complete and gathered by chapter. Spurgeon returned to this book more often, and more tenderly, than to almost any other — reading it as a love-song between Christ and his people, and tracing that love through every season the soul knows: nearness and absence, longing and rest, the dark night and the breaking day. Each sermon is freely available to download.
