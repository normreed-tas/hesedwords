JONAH  ·  CHAPTER TWO  ·  VERSE EIGHT
מְשַׁמְּרִים הַבְלֵי־שָׁוְא חַסְדָּם יַעֲזֹבוּ
meshamerim  ·  havlei-shav  ·  chasdam  ·  ya'azovu
Those who cling to worthless idols
forfeit the hesed that is
already theirs.
· · ·
הֶבֶל
Hevel
Breath. Vapour.
What looks solid
but dissolves when grasped.
חֶסֶד
Hesed
Covenant faithfulness.
Loyal love. Not given
from outside — already yours.
יַעֲזֹבוּ
Ya'azovu
To forsake. Desert.
Not stolen from you —
walked away from yourself.
A DIAGNOSTIC FOR THE SECOND PHASE
Is this flowing from being — or has the doing begun to consume the being again?
Am I sent into this work, or pulled by the weight of significance?
Can I hold this loosely enough to walk away without grief
disproportionate to the plant?
The grace you hold but refuse to extend — you eventually can no longer hold at all.
Not to do, but to be.
