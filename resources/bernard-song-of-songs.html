Bernard of Clairvaux on the Song of Songs

Bernard preached these eighty-six sermons to the monks of Clairvaux across more than two decades, returning again and again to a single book as though it could never be exhausted. He read the Song neither as history nor as allegory in the loose sense, but as the record of an experience available, in some measure, to anyone who has tasted what he calls the kiss of Christ. He never reached beyond the third chapter; he died while writing his eighty-sixth sermon, mid-argument, the work genuinely unfinished. What survives — all of it, now gathered below — is the longest sustained meditation on divine intimacy in Western monastic literature.
