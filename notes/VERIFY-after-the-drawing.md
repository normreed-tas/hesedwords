## Attributions

**17. Spurgeon, Sermon 2294.** **CONFIRMED** against the passage recorded in the sequence note: "All my brothers and sisters will run at once if You do draw me." Capitals and punctuation match.

**18. Augustine, *Confessions* I.1.** **CONFIRMED.** Pilkington: "our hearts are restless until they rest in You". The Latin is singular (*cor nostrum*), so the draft's "our heart is restless until it rests in God" is a fair paraphrase.

**20. Lewis, *The Weight of Glory*.** **NOT OPENED** (in copyright). The sermon does speak of "desire for our own far off country" and "news from a country we have never yet visited". "A longing that nothing in this world quite satisfies" is closer to *Mere Christianity* than to the sermon; as a paraphrase of Lewis it is fair.

**21. Lewis, *Mere Christianity*.** **CONFIRMED** from a secondary source: "If I find in myself a desire which no experience in this world can satisfy, the most probable explanation is that I was made for another world." The paraphrase is fair.

**22. *Chariots of Fire*.** The primary was not opened. *The Treasure* (footnote 3) already carries the line and its source: the 1981 screenplay by Colin Welland, with no record that Liddell said it. In the film he says it to his sister Jennie. *The Treasure* prints "his pleasure", lower case; the draft has "His".
