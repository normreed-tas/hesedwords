# VERIFY — *There It Is Again* (reflection, Psalm 21:7)

Responds to `there-it-is-again.verify.md` (prose locked 25 November 2026). Checked by Claude Code, 25 November 2026, against the page as uploaded.

**Sources opened:** ESV, KJV, NKJV and NASB 1995 on Bible Hub (each verse page of Psalm 21:1–9, and the ESV chapter page for the title); the Hebrew of Psalm 21 (OSHB).

**In short:** one fidelity fix (item 10, "lovingkindness" without a hyphen). Everything else is confirmed. Published.

| # | Item | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ps 21:2 (part) | **CONFIRMED.** ESV: "You have given him his heart's desire and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah". Quoting the first half alone is fair. |
| 2 | Ps 21:3 (part) | **CONFIRMED** in wording. ESV: "For you meet him with rich blessings; you set a crown of fine gold upon his head." The page's capital "You" is its own, because the line stands alone. Left as it is. |
| 3 | Ps 21:4 (part) | **CONFIRMED.** ESV: "He asked life of you; you gave it to him, length of days forever and ever." Fair as a part. |
| 4 | Ps 21:7 | **CONFIRMED**, word for word, with the ESV's line break after "LORD,". The site sets LORD in plain capitals. |
| 5 | "For six verses David tells God…" | **CONFIRMED.** Title: "To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David." (לְדָוִד). Verses 1–6 are all addressed to God ("your strength", "you have given", "you bestow"). |
| 6 | "A king's psalm" | **CONFIRMED.** "The king" in verses 1 and 7. |
| 7 | "After this verse the psalm turns to the king's enemies" | **CONFIRMED.** 21:8: "Your hand will find out all your enemies". |
| 8 | "The long life" | **CONFIRMED.** 21:4: "length of days forever and ever". |
| 9 | *Hesed* in 21:7 | **CONFIRMED.** וּבְחֶסֶד עֶלְיוֹן, verse 8 in the Hebrew numbering. |
| 10 | "loving-kindness" or "mercy" | **CONFIRMED and FIXED.** KJV and NKJV: "through the mercy of the most High". NASB 1995: "through the lovingkindness of the Most High". The NASB spells it as one word, and so does the rest of the site, so the hyphen was removed. |
