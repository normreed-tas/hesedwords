# HELD — *yaqar*: Bomana

**Held.** Seed for a possible future article. Not a draft, and not for publication.

**This note is deliberately spare.** The personal material behind it is not in the repo and
should not be put there. Everything in this repository is public, and this material is not
Norm's alone to publish: it involves Peter Gill's widow and children. Norm holds the full
account outside the repo. Before any of it is written for the site, he speaks to Molly.

## The seed

- Peter Gill, mission pilot in Papua New Guinea, flying **P2-UIF**. He and his family
  arrived in PNG in the mid-1980s.
- Peter and Molly Gill were close friends of Norm and his wife. The two families' children
  were of similar ages. Norm flew often with Peter.
- **1992:** the aircraft crashed in the PNG highlands. Peter and all on board were killed.
- A hard loss for both families, and a lasting mark on the church and the mission.
- **Psalm 116:15** — *Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of his saints* — was
  put on Peter's headstone at **Bomana**, Port Moresby.

## Why it is held

The verse on the stone is a pricing, made by people standing at a grave, and it is the same
word the *yaqar* study is about. That is why it ends that study.

But Norm carries more of this than the stone, and that material is larger than a word
study's final paragraph. It is a reflection in its own right, not an illustration. Held
here so it is not spent.

## Where the pieces stand

- **How Precious** (reflection, Psalm 36:7, 2 November 2026) — *yaqar* as a valuer's word.
  Does not touch Bomana.
- ***Yaqar*** (word study, 2 November 2026) — ends at the stone, and no further. The telling
  and the identifying stay out by Norm's decision of 2 November 2026. Prose final and
  verified; companion line to *How Precious* both ways.
- **Future article** — unwritten, unscheduled, and gated on the conversation with Molly.

## Loose ends

- An aircraft already appears on the site, in *God Is Searching*: "aircraft to bring the
  delegates into a remote airstrip", and "the aircraft became a nightmare, and stayed one,
  until a former missionary… rang out of the blue". **Corrected 2 November 2026:** an
  earlier version of this note quoted "the aircraft in Papua New Guinea he did not arrange",
  which is the site index's card summary, not the page. Whether that aircraft is P2-UIF is
  Norm's to say. Any new piece returns to this ground knowingly.
- No claim about the crash should be verified against outside sources without asking Norm
  first. It is his friend's death, not a fact to be audited.
