## History and attribution

**24. Tacitus, *Annals* 15.44.** **CONFIRMED.** "Covered with the skins of beasts, they were torn by dogs and perished, or were nailed to crosses, or were doomed to the flames and burnt, to serve as a nightly illumination… Nero offered his gardens for the spectacle."

**25. Pliny, *Letters* 10.96.** **CONFIRMED.** Those who "invoked the gods… offered prayer with incense and wine to your image… and moreover cursed Christ… these I thought should be discharged." "Fifty years later" is 48 by the usual dates. Fair.

**26. Lewis, "locked on the inside".** **NOT OPENED.** *The Problem of Pain* is in copyright and no text of it could be opened. A secondary quotation was seen with "door", singular; the usual citation is "the doors of hell are locked on the inside", chapter 8 ("Hell"), 1940. Norm may wish to check his own copy.

**27. Eusebius.** **CONFIRMED.** 2.25: "Paul was beheaded in Rome itself, and… Peter likewise was crucified under Nero", citing Tertullian, Caius and Dionysius of Corinth. 3.1: Peter "having come to Rome… was crucified head-downwards", from Origen. "Drawing on earlier writers" is fair.
