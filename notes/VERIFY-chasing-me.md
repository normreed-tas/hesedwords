## Claims about the text

| # | Claim | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Pharaoh's army pursued Israel to the sea | **CONFIRMED.** Exod 14:9: "The Egyptians pursued them… and overtook them encamped at the sea". The verb is רדף (וַיִּרְדְּפוּ). |
| 6 | 1 Sam 26:13 | **CONFIRMED.** "stood far off on the top of the hill, with a great space between them". "A wide space" is a fair restatement. |
| 7 | 1 Sam 26:18 uses *radaph* | **CONFIRMED**: רֹדֵף אַחֲרֵי עַבְדּוֹ. |
| 8 | Third person, then "you" | **CONFIRMED.** Verses 1–3 speak of God as "he"; verse 4 has "you are with me". |
| 9 | David keeping the sheep | **CONFIRMED.** 1 Sam 16:11: "he is keeping the sheep". |

## Hebrew

| # | Claim | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Ps 23:6 uses *radaph* | **CONFIRMED**: יִרְדְּפוּנִי, "they will pursue me". |
| 11 | The ordinary sense is pursuit | **CONFIRMED** from use. The verb stands 73 times in the Hebrew Bible in the text opened, and its uses checked here are all a chase (Exod 14:9; 1 Sam 26:18). No lexicon was opened. |
| 12 | "Mercy" is *hesed* | **CONFIRMED**: וָחֶסֶד. |
| 13 | Goodness first, then mercy | **CONFIRMED**: טוֹב וָחֶסֶד, in that order. |
| 14 | *Attah immadi* has no verb | **CONFIRMED**: כִּי־אַתָּה עִמָּדִי, a pronoun and "with me". English supplies "are". |
| 16 | "It does not say they will catch me" | **CONFIRMED.** Ps 23:6 has רדף alone. Exod 14:9 shows the pair it lacks: "pursued" (רדף) and then "overtook" (נשׂג). |
