# VERIFY — *Hidden* (article, Ephesians 3:1–13)

Answers `hidden.verify.md` (18 November 2026). Checked by Claude Code, 18 November 2026. Sources: WEB on Bible Hub, verse by verse; SBLGNT; LXX Judith with Brenton (Ellopos); Precept Austin's Ephesians 3:10 page, which reproduces Wuest; web search results for the Lewis chapter title.

| # | Item | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Judith 2:2 | **CORRECTED.** The LXX has τὸ μυστήριον τῆς βουλῆς αὐτοῦ, "the mystery of his **counsel**" (Brenton: "his secret counsel"), at a war council of officers and nobles. Eph 1:9 has τὸ μυστήριον τοῦ **θελήματος** αὐτοῦ. So these are not "the same words". It now reads "the mystery of his counsel"… "Paul uses almost the same phrase in Ephesians 1:9". |
| Q2 | Lewis | **CORRECTED capitalisation**: "Deeper Magic from Before the Dawn of Time". It is the **title of chapter 15**, and the footnote now says so. Several study guides agree on this. **The book itself was not opened.** |
| Q3 | Wuest, "much-variegated wisdom of God" | **CONFIRMED** as reproduced by Precept Austin, credited to Eerdmans. Year not confirmed, so the footnote is left without one. The Expanded Translation itself was not opened. |
| Q4 | "university for angels" | **FOUND, and adjusted.** Wuest, on Eph 3:10, as reproduced by Precept Austin: "The Church thus becomes the 'university' for angels, and each saint a 'professor'… 1Pe 1:12 speaks of the things which the angels have a passionate desire to stoop down and look into". He says it of **the Church**, with 1 Peter 1:12 alongside. The sentence now reads: Wuest called the church a "university" for angels. The volume is presumably his Word Studies on Ephesians, **not opened**. |
| WEB | All listed verses | **CONFIRMED**, with six small corrections to match the WEB: Eph 3:6 (no comma after "fellow heirs"); Eph 3:21 ("to all generations, forever and ever"); Eph 1:21 ("rule, authority, power, dominion"); Gen 12:3 ("All the families of the earth will be blessed through you"); Col 2:3 ("all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden"); Job 1:8 ("my servant, Job"). |
| V1 | *mystērion* three times in Eph 3 | **CONFIRMED**: 3:3, 3:4, 3:9. Also 1:9, 5:32, 6:19. |
| V3 | *elachistoteros* | **CONFIRMED**: ἐλαχιστοτέρῳ, a comparative built on the superlative. |
| V4 | Three *syn-* words in 3:6 | **CONFIRMED**: συγκληρονόμα, σύσσωμα, συμμέτοχα. |
| V5 | "in the heavenly places" | **CONFIRMED**: ἐν τοῖς ἐπουρανίοις at Eph 1:3, 1:20, 2:6, 3:10, 6:12. The exact phrase occurs nowhere else in the NT. |
| V6 | *polypoikilos* | **CONFIRMED**: only here in the NT. Hoehner (via Precept): outside the NT "it refers to intricate embroidery or flowers of many colors". |
| V7 | *archai*, *exousiai* at 6:12 | **CONFIRMED**, the same pair as at 3:10. |
| V8 | *theatron*, 1 Cor 4:9 | **CONFIRMED**. |
| V9 | *methodeia* | **CONFIRMED**: Eph 4:14 and 6:11 only. |
| V10 | stand ×4 | **CONFIRMED**: στῆναι (6:11), ἀντιστῆναι and στῆναι (6:13), στῆτε (6:14). |
| V2, V11 | English "mystery" from *mystērion*; 3:20–21 ends the first half | Standard. |
| Q5, Q6 | Norm's own material | Not checked, by design. |
