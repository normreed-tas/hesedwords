# If You Knew — held note

**Held.** Not a piece yet. The article on John 4 goes ahead; this note is the book idea
set aside so it is not lost, and so the article does not quietly become the book.

## The idea

*If you knew the gift of God, and who it is that is saying to you, Give me a drink, you
would have asked him, and he would have given you living water.* (John 4:10)

The sentence is contrary to fact — she does not know, and so she did not ask. The
not-knowing shows up in the asking. She asks for water that saves her the walk. Her
request is modest, reasonable, and far too small, and she has no idea.

The book would not be on John 4. Forty verses will not carry a book, and a book made of
one observation wears out by the third chapter. The frame that might carry it is wider:
*if you knew* is not a John 4 sentence. It is the charge John lays again and again, and
it tightens as the Gospel goes on.

## The spine

- 1:10 — the world knew him not
- 1:26 — among you stands one whom you do not know
- 3:10 — are you the teacher of Israel, and do not know these things?
- 4:10 — if you knew the gift of God
- 4:32 — I have food you know nothing about (to the disciples)
- 8:19 — you know neither me nor my Father
- 14:9 — have I been so long with you, and you have not known me, Philip?

The world, then the Baptist's hearers, then the teacher of Israel, then the Samaritan,
then the Twelve, then one named friend on the last night. The circle closes inward.

progression can be checked rather than asserted. The claim that it tightens inward is the
whole idea; if the occurrences do not actually run that way, there is no book.

## Why it might vary rather than repeat

Each instance asks a different question: what do you do with not-knowing when you are a
pagan, a scholar, an outsider, a friend? John 4 would be the hinge — the one place where
the not-knowing is answered rather than merely exposed. 14:9 would be the last chapter,
and it earns the ending because it is not a rebuke of outsiders. The charge lands on the
man who has given everything to following him.

*Reading-within-frame* — the progression as a deliberate authorial structure. John
clearly returns to the theme; that it is arranged as a closing circle is a reading, not
something the text states.

## What the article takes, and the book must not take back

The article has beats on: the grammar of 4:10; the small request; Nicodemus asking *how*
rather than *give me*; gift named before giver, and why the spiral not the switch; the
disciples' matching ignorance at 4:32; and 14:9 as the closing turn.

If the book is ever written, those stay in the article. The book would need its own route
through John 4 — most likely much shorter, since the article will have done it.

## Already on this ground

- **Is There No Hesed in John?** (2 Aug 2026, article) — John 1:14, 1:17, 13:1-17. Argues
  that *grace and truth* is *hesed we'emet* in Greek. The book would rest on this and not
  re-argue it.
- **Knowing Where He Came From** (21 Sep 2026, article) — John 13:3. Knowing who you are
  is what makes the service possible, not the obstacle humility overcomes. This is the
  same thread running the other way: his knowing enables; our not-knowing cripples the
  asking. A book on knowing in John would have to hold both, and this piece is probably
  the better doorway to that than John 4 is.
- **Not the Lesson I First Thought** (19 Jul 2026, reflection) — Ezra 4; John 4. Takes the
  mountain-and-temple grievance. That part of John 4 is spent.

## Where it stalled

Nowhere, yet — the decision was simply that the article comes first and the book declares
itself or does not. Two things would settle it:

1. Whether the occurrence list survives being read rather than recalled.
2. Whether there is a Selah for it. There is none at present. The article will have its
   own; a book needs more than one, and none of this has yet been paid for out of Norm's
   own life.

The honest test is whether the material keeps generating under his hands while the article
is being written. Books that start as *there might be enough here* tend to show it.
