## 1. Quotations (ESV)

Confirmed word for word, allowing a capital where the draft starts its own sentence: Titus 1:5; 1:7–8; 1:10; 1:11 (both phrases); 1:12; 1:16; 2:5; 2:7–8; 2:11–12; 2:14; 3:1 (the ellipsis is fair); 3:5; 3:8; 3:14; 1 Corinthians 9:27; 1 Timothy 4:12; Galatians 1:14 (the ellipsis covers "was I"); Exodus 19:5; Matthew 5:16; Matthew 6:3.

| Ref | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Acts 14:27** | **NOT word for word.** ESV: "And when they arrived and gathered the church together, they declared all that God had done with them". The draft joins it as "they gathered the church together and declared all that…". Suggested: they "gathered the church together" and "declared all that God had done with them", as two fragments. |
| **Titus 2:10** | The ESV text opened reads "God our **Savior**". The draft has "Saviour". |

| **Titus 1:7** | The draft says "An elder must not be…". The ESV has "an **overseer**" in 1:7 (elders are named in 1:5). The quoted words themselves are exact. For Norm: say "overseer", or leave it, since Paul is describing the same men. |

**Spelling practice.** The site has no earlier ESV quotation with "Savior" or "Saviour". *What God Cannot Do* (26 November) kept the anglicised "defence" in an ESV quotation. The anglicised ESV does read "Saviour" and "practising", so the draft's spellings are a real edition's and are consistent with that page.

## 3. Julian

**CONFIRMED.** Letter 22, *To Arsacius, High-priest of Galatia*, dated 362 by the translator. It opens "The Hellenic religion does not yet prosper as I desire", and says: "it is disgraceful that, when no Jew ever has to beg, and the impious Galilaeans support not only their own poor but ours as well, all men see that our people lack aid from us."

Citation for the footnote: Julian, Letter 22, "To Arsacius, High-priest of Galatia" (AD 362), in *The Works of the Emperor Julian*, translated by W. C. Wright, Loeb Classical Library, volume 3. The volume number is from the brief and general knowledge, not read off the page opened.

"In the fourth century" and "who wanted the old gods back" both hold.

## 4. Repo facts

**The earlier article.** *Our God-Sponsored Projects* does speak of sheep churches and goat churches, and of the list. But the draft's summary is sharper than the article on one point. The draft says of a goat church: "It's all on the website." The article says: "The website is not the problem", and "A sheep church might well list the food van and the prison visits, because to a sheep church that is not an achievement". Its test is the minister who can produce the list. **For Norm:** the opening paragraph, and the later paragraph on a church starting a website "so people will notice", may read as going further than the earlier piece did.
