# VERIFY — *For Your Hesed's Sake* (article, Psalm 6)

Answers `for-your-hesed-sake.verify.md` (15 November 2026). Checked by Claude Code, 15 November 2026. Norm asked for it to be published now.

| # | Item | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–12 | ESV quotations (Ps 6:1–10, Ex 34:6, Mt 4:17, Ps 143:12) | **CONFIRMED**. Ps 143 has 12 verses, so 143:12 is the last. | ESV on Bible Hub; WLC |
| 13 | "ESV renders both as 'restore'" | **CORRECTED.** Lam 5:21 ESV: "Restore us to yourself… that we may be restored"; Jer 31:18 ESV: "**bring me back** that I may be restored". Now reads: "The ESV renders them 'restore us' and 'bring me back', so the English reader doesn't see the same verb used twice." | ESV |
| 14 | 6:5 (Heb) | **CONFIRMED**: *shuvah* (7725), three imperatives (shuvah, challetsah, hoshi'eni), *lema'an chasdekha*. | WLC |

| 18 | No confession | **CONFIRMED.** No word for sin or for confessing. *yodeh* (6:6 Heb, from *yadah*) means "give praise" in context. *aven* (6:9 Heb) is the enemies' "evil". | WLC |

| 24 | Luther's first book | **CONFIRMED**: "The first edition of Martin Luther's first book… the seven penitential Psalms (Psalms 6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, and 143)… published in March 1517". The 1516 *Theologia Deutsch* was an edition of another author's work, so this is fair (from general knowledge, not opened). "A young monk": he was an Augustinian friar of about 33. | Emory Pitts Theology Library, item 8544 |
| 25 | "Seven months later" | **Note:** Emory says "**six months** before his 95 Theses". March to 31 October is seven months by counting, so either is defensible. Left as written. | Emory |

Fair paraphrases: "keep God happy so he will continue to bless you"; "Jesus earned our acceptance… In the gospel we simply receive it"; "in the gospel there is ultimately a sweetness". Copyright © 2009 Timothy Keller, Redeemer City to City. 2009 − 1517 = 492, so "nearly five hundred years" holds. | cru.org, "All of Life is Repentance" |
