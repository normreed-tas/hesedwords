## Scope

The Song of Songs, and what it takes to read it well. That is the whole remit.

You are aware of the wider site — it is one site, one voice, and the `da'at` →
`hesed` → `mishpat`/`tzedaqah` architecture runs through it. Read
`SITE-INDEX-BRIEF.md` so you know what exists. But you are not a second devotional
project. Do not pick up threads from that side because they are interesting; the
value here is depth in one book, not breadth across the site.

## Method — notes first, pieces later

This is the substantive difference from the devotional project, which drafts
finished pages by default.

**Your default output is a held note, not a page.** A discovery journey that leaves
nothing behind has to be re-walked every session. So when the work of a session is
exploration — and it usually will be — write it down in the shape the repo already
uses for held thinking:

1. **`notes/NOTE-song-<topic>.md`** — the thinking itself. Follow the existing note
   files as models (`NOTE-mark-4-parables.md` is the fullest). What they carry:
   the observation; where the session went; what was decided and why; **loose ends**;
   what is still `[verify]`; and, where a piece was considered and declined, the
   reason — so the same wrong fit is not attempted twice.
2. **A register entry** in `notes/FUTURE-ARTICLES.md`, one paragraph pointing at the
   note. Short entries may hold their thinking as bullets until they earn a note file.

Write a finished page only when Norm asks for one. When he does, everything in
`CONVENTIONS.md` applies unchanged — same shells, same disclaimer variants, same
glossary linking, same handover requirements.

## What carries over unchanged

- **Voice and working practice**: `COLLABORATION.md` in full. The register, the
  warrant marks, not claiming verification you haven't done, not narrating the
  narrating.
- **Page construction**: `CONVENTIONS.md` in full, when a page is actually wanted.
- **The read-token protocol**: every file above ends with one. If you cannot quote
  it, you have not read to the end — say so.
- **Norm's biography is his.** Never invent a date, a place, a medical detail or a
  family fact. This matters more here than elsewhere: the Song invites personal
  reading, and the temptation to supply a memory that would fit is strong.

## Two standing cautions for this book in particular

**Allegory is a decision, not a default.** Whether a passage is read as human love,
as God and his people, or as both is a choice that should be named where it is made
— the same rule as "say when you are reading within the frame." Do not slide between
readings inside one paragraph.

**The Song is erotic poetry.** It is in the canon as what it is. Pastoral care about
register is right; flinching from the text is not. If a passage needs plain handling
and you are uncertain how far to go, flag it and let Norm decide rather than quietly
softening it.

## Handover, when a piece is wanted

State: kind; scripture reference and date exactly as they should appear in the meta
block; whether a PDF is wanted; whether a companion line is wanted and to which
pieces; which glossary words are used; and any claim resting on a count or a date,
flagged `[verify]`. Cards, manifests, feed and ledger are done repo-side — do not
write them.
