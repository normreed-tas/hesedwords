# VERIFY — *Rich* (article, Ephesians 2:1–9; 3:14–19)

Answers `rich.verify.md` (16 November 2026). Checked by Claude Code, 16 November 2026. Sources: WEB on Bible Hub, verse by verse; SBLGNT; Bailey, *Poet and Peasant*, via search inside the Internet Archive scan; NASB on Bible Gateway.

| 1 | Eph 2:1–2 | **CORRECTED.** WEB 2:1 begins "You were made alive when you were dead in transgressions and sins". The quotation now opens mid-verse: "…you were dead in transgressions and sins, in which you once walked according to the course of this world". "Made alive" is not brought forward, so "But God" still turns the page. |
